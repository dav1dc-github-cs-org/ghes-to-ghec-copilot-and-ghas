# Briefing notes: engine failure after V1

**Objective.** Continue the take-off after an engine failure above V1, fly the engine-out
departure and return for an overweight landing.

**Instructor setup.**

- Load `engine-failure-after-v1`. The engine 1 flameout is scheduled at 38 s, a few knots
  after V1 at this weight.
- Weather: 250/12, 9999, BKN025, 18/11, QNH 1009.

**Standards.**

- Directional control within 10 m of the centreline during the continued take-off.
- V2 to V2 + 10 kt until acceleration altitude.
- Checklist actions in order, without prompting.

**Debrief points.**

- Rudder input timing, and the heading excursion in the first three seconds.
- Speed discipline in the second segment.
- Decision-making for the overweight landing.
